# Regional art direction references

Reviewed 8 October 2026. These are original artistic environments, not measured architectural reconstructions. A region contains many traditions; a selected house or landscape must not be presented as representing all its communities. No ritual objects, deities or sacred textile patterns were traced. Architecture is simplified in the SVG scenes; the Javanese plate is generated original artwork.

| World | Visual cue and boundary | Research reference |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Jawa | Tiered joglo roof, open wooden pavilion, morning garden | [Indonesia Travel: Joglo](https://www.indonesia.travel/id/id/travel-ideas/heritage/joglo-traditional-house) |
| Sunda | Bamboo and mountain landscape; no claim of reproducing a ceremonial house | [Traditional architecture overview](https://kebudayaan.kemdikbud.go.id/dpk/arsitektur-vernakular/) |
| Bali | Water-garden landscape and stone framing; no shrine or deity ornaments | [Balinese Traditional Landscape research](https://download.garuda.kemdikbud.go.id/article.php?article=1341487&title=BALINESE+TRADITIONAL+LANDSCAPE&val=928) |
| Minang | Curving roof silhouette and highland setting | [Indonesia Travel: traditional houses](https://www.indonesia.travel/id/id/travel-ideas/heritage/9-iconic-traditional-houses-to-explore-in-indonesia) |
| Betawi | Wide veranda and pitched roof; no borrowed Javanese ritual motifs | [Indonesia Travel: Rumah Kebaya](https://www.indonesia.travel/id/id/travel-ideas/culture/rumah-kebaya-betawi) |
| Bugis | Sailing vessel and shoreline, maritime rather than temple architecture | [Indonesia Travel: Pinisi](https://www.indonesia.travel/us/en/destination/sulawesi/south-sulawesi/makassar---pinisi-schooner) |
| Melayu | River-palace interpretation; not a replica of a specific palace | [Indonesia Travel: Istana Siak](https://www.indonesia.travel/id/id/destination/sumatra/riau/istana-siak-sri-indrapura) |
| Palembang | Limas roof profile and riverside composition; generic woven geometry | [Indonesia Travel: Rumah Limas](https://www.indonesia.travel/id/en/destination/sumatra/south-sumatra/rumah-limas-palembang) |
| Batak | Raised house and curved roof near Lake Toba | [Indonesia Travel: Rumah Bolon](https://www.indonesia.travel/id/id/travel-ideas/heritage/rumah-bolon) |
| Aceh | Elevated wooden house, veranda and stairs; no decorative religious text | [Indonesia Travel: Rumoh Aceh](https://www.indonesia.travel/id/id/travel-ideas/heritage/rumoh-aceh) |
| Dayak | Long raised house inspired specifically by Radakng form | [Indonesia Travel: Radakng](https://www.indonesia.travel/id/en/travel-ideas/heritage/radakng-traditional-house) |

| Toraja | Curved Tongkonan roof silhouette; no ceremonial animal imagery | [Indonesia Travel: Tongkonan](https://www.indonesia.travel/id/id/travel-ideas/heritage/tongkonan-traditional-house) |
| Madura | Row of homes around a long open family courtyard | [Tanean Lanjhang research, IAIN Madura](https://ejournal.iainmadura.ac.id/index.php/islamuna/article/download/4414/2814) |
| Lampung | Stilted veranda and welcoming stair arrangement | [Indonesia Travel: Nuwo Sesat](https://www.indonesia.travel/id/id/travel-ideas/heritage/nuwo-sesat-traditional-house) |
| Maluku | Island silhouettes and ocean light; landscape-led identity | [Indonesia Travel: Maluku](https://www.indonesia.travel/gb/en/destination/maluku-papua/maluku) |
| Papua | Highland forest interpretation; not a pan-Papuan motif collection | [Indonesia Travel: Honai context](https://www.indonesia.travel/id/id/travel-ideas/culture/honai-traditional-house-southwest-papua) |
| NTT | Sumba-inspired tall thatched roof with savanna, explicitly named in description | [Indonesia Travel: Sumba house context](https://www.indonesia.travel/gb/en/travel-ideas/heritage/9-iconic-traditional-houses-to-explore-in-indonesia) |
| Nusantara | Abstract island horizon; no mixing of ceremonial symbols | Original geographic interpretation, not an ethnographic representation |

For a future collection with detailed carvings or named ceremonial textiles, commission review from practitioners of the specific tradition before adding those motifs.
